Mutual exclusion allows only one processor at a time to enter a shared critical section.
We study a sequential model of the voting stage in Rabin’s mutual-exclusion algorithm <Citation {...references.algorithm} />.
In each *voting round*, every contender flips a fair coin to decide whether to withdraw <Citation {...references.voting} locator={undefined} />.
One survivor wins, several survivors vote again, and no survivors means that the election has failed.
How likely is voting to end with no survivors?

## The Program

The outer loop runs the voting rounds.
The inner loop makes one withdrawal decision for each contender present at the start of the round.
The variable `i` counts contenders who have not withdrawn, and `n` counts decisions still to be made in the round.

## Verification {#success-bound}

For `init_i > 1`, the probability of terminating with no survivors is at most $1/3$.

<span id="what-the-bound-means" />

The [`@wlp` annotation](../../proof-rules/approximations.md#calculus-annotations) verifies the complementary lower bound of $2/3$, counting both one survivor and nontermination.
It does not establish that voting terminates.

<span id="how-the-proof-works" />

The outer [`@invariant`](../../proof-rules/induction.md) annotation uses $I=[i=1]+\tfrac23\cdot[i>1]$.

A round starting with $k\ge2$ contenders leaves zero or one survivors with probabilities $2^{-k}$ and $k\cdot2^{-k}$, respectively.
Its expected invariant value is therefore

$$
k\cdot2^{-k}
+\frac23\cdot\left(1-(k+1)\cdot2^{-k}\right)
=\frac23+\frac{k-2}{3\cdot2^k}
\ge\frac23.
$$

For `n <= i`, the inner invariant uses `rec_pown_2(n)` for $2^{-n}$.

- $([i=n]\cdot n+[i=n+1])\cdot2^{-n}$ gives the probability that the round ends with one survivor.
  With `i == n`, one remaining contender must stay, whereas with `i == n + 1`, all must withdraw.
- $\tfrac23\cdot(1-([i=n]\cdot(n+1)+[i=n+1])\cdot2^{-n})$ accounts for rounds with at least two survivors.
- `[n <= i]` restricts the calculation to states with enough contenders for the remaining flips.
